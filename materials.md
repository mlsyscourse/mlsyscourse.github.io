## Materials on Deep Learning


- [Deep Learning Book](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) is a comprehensive material for the field.
- [Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/) Interactive deep learning book with code, math, and discussions.
- [CS231n](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/) is a great course to get started on DL for computer vision.
